GOV.UK One Login helps people sign in and prove their identity so they can access government services quickly and securely.

This section explains how to apply the GOV.UK One Login brand consistently across digital products, communications and channels.

It provides guidance on using:

- logos
- symbols
- colour
- service headers

Using the GOV.UK One Login brand consistently will strengthen trust, improve recognition
and ensure users can move confidently between government services.
